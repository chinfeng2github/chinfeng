## 🧑‍💻 About Me

> 15+ years of full-stack experience, with the last 10+ years focused on microservice architecture, distributed systems governance, and developer productivity.

I'm a seasoned full-stack engineer based in **Chengdu, China**, with a strong track record of delivering production-grade systems. Over the past 14+ years, I've been a technical backbone leading the complete evolution of core systems from monolithic to Spring Cloud microservice architecture.

I love building things end-to-end — from architecting distributed backends and setting up CI/CD pipelines, to developing cross-platform mobile apps and managing cloud infrastructure.

### ✨ What I Do

- 🏗️ **Architecture & Backend** — Spring Cloud microservice design, distributed systems, high-availability patterns
- 📱 **Mobile & Frontend** — React Native, Flutter, Vue3, Tauri2 — full product lifecycle from design to App Store
- ⚙️ **DevOps & Infrastructure** — Docker, Ansible, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, ELK stack, multi-cloud resource management
- 🤖 **AI Integration** — ComfyUI workflows, Dify AI pipelines, LLM-powered features
- 🛠️ **Cross-Platform** — Independently developed and shipped 3 apps on iOS & Android
